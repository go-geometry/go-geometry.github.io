What it will cover

Scope: robust predicates, convex hulls, Delaunay, Voronoi, k-d trees, mesh generation.

Nothing here is released. This is the scope of the plan, as PLAN.md states it: item 7 of wave 2 (exact predicates, Quickhull, Delaunay, Voronoi, alpha shapes, k-d trees) and, in wave 3, item 13 (meshing).

∎

Exact predicates

Adaptive-precision orient2d, orient3d, incircle and insphere: the exact sign at close to floating-point cost. Planned first, because every other item silently builds invalid topology without them.

△

Hulls, Delaunay, Voronoi

Quickhull in n dimensions, Delaunay triangulations in 2D and 3D, Voronoi diagrams and alpha shapes: the MATLAB convhull, delaunay, voronoi and alphaShape surface.

⌖

Nearest neighbours

k-d trees with exact neighbour search and a MATLAB-style knnsearch, adopting gonum's trees rather than rewriting them.

▦

Mesh generation

Constrained Delaunay triangulation with Ruppert refinement in 2D, then 3D, in wave 3: the meshes go-fem solves on.

🎯

Robust by construction

Hulls and triangulations are never shipped before the predicates they depend on, and are validated with the exact predicates, not with floating-point ones.

⚖️

Licences kept clean

Triangle, TetGen, CGAL and Gmsh are oracles only, run as external programs; nothing of them is ported.
